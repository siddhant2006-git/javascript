About the Day

A Century of Voices, Rising

What began as a labor movement in 1911 has grown into a worldwide observance — a day to look back at how far we've come, and look ahead at the distance still to travel.

I.

Where It Began

Born from early 20th-century labor movements demanding fair wages, voting rights, and better conditions for women across the globe.

II.

What It Means Today

A day of recognition and reflection — celebrating achievement while spotlighting the work still needed to close gaps in pay, leadership, and opportunity.

III.

Why It Matters

Progress is not automatic. Every conversation, every policy, every act of mentorship moves the needle — this day exists to keep that momentum alive.
